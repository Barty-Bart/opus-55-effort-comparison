How to Play

Goal

Build an economy, advance from the Dark Age through the Feudal and Castle ages to the Imperial Age, raise an army and destroy every enemy unit and building. Alternatively, build a Wonder and defend it for 5 minutes.

Economy

Villagers gather food (sheep, deer, boar, berries, fish, farms), wood, gold and stone and carry it to a drop-off: the Town Center, a Mill (food), Lumber Camp (wood) or Mining Camp (gold, stone). Build Houses to raise your population limit. Keep your Town Center producing villagers!

Ages

Advance at the Town Center. Each age needs two buildings of the previous age (e.g. Barracks + Mill for the Feudal Age) and unlocks new units, buildings and technologies.

Combat

Spearmen beat cavalry · cavalry beat archers · archers beat infantry · skirmishers beat archers · rams and trebuchets destroy buildings · monks heal and convert. Research upgrades at the Blacksmith.
