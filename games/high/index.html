Age of Kingdoms

A medieval real-time strategy game

How to Play

Goal

Destroy every enemy unit and building — or build a Wonder in the Imperial Age and defend it until its timer runs out.

Economy

Villagers gather food (sheep, berries, deer, farms), wood, gold and stone. Build a Lumber Camp, Mining Camp or Mill next to resources so villagers walk less. Build Houses to raise your population limit.

Ages

Advance at the Town Center: Dark → Feudal → Castle → Imperial. Each age needs two buildings from the previous age and unlocks new units, buildings and technologies.

Mouse

Left click / drag select · Double click select all of a type · Right click move, gather, build, attack, set rally point · Wheel zoom · Middle drag pan

Keyboard

Arrows or screen edge — scroll
Q W E R T / A S D F G / Z X C V B — command buttons
H Town Center · . idle villager · , idle military
Ctrl+1–9 make group · 1–9 select group
Space centre on selection · Del delete
Shift queue buildings / add to selection · Alt+right-click garrison
+ / − game speed · P pause · Esc cancel / menu

Tips

Spearmen beat cavalry, archers beat spearmen, skirmishers beat archers, cavalry beat archers, rams destroy buildings. Ring the Town Bell to garrison villagers and fire extra arrows.
