Age of Kingdoms

Grow a settlement. Advance through the ages. Crush your rival.

How to Play

Goal

Destroy the enemy's Town Centers, Castles, villagers and army. Lose yours and you are defeated.

Economy

Villagers gather food (sheep, berries, deer, farms), wood, gold and stone. Build Lumber Camps, Mining Camps and Mills close to resources to cut walking time. Houses raise your population limit.

Ages

Research the next Age at the Town Center to unlock buildings, units and technologies: Dark → Feudal → Castle → Imperial.

Army

Spearmen beat cavalry, skirmishers beat archers, archers beat infantry, knights crush archers. Rams demolish buildings. Research Blacksmith upgrades!

Mouse

  • Left click / drag — select
  • Double click — select all of that type on screen
  • Right click — move, gather, build, attack, set rally point
  • Wheel — zoom · Middle drag — pan

Keyboard

  • Arrows / screen edge — scroll camera
  • Q W E R T / A S D F G / Z X C V B — command grid
  • H — Town Center · . — idle villager · , — idle army
  • Ctrl+1…9 set group · 1…9 recall group
  • Shift — add to selection / queue ×5 / keep placing
  • Del — delete · P — pause · Space — jump to alert · Esc — cancel / menu
