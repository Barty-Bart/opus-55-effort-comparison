How to Play

Goal

Grow your settlement, advance from the Dark Age through the Feudal and Castle Ages to the Imperial Age, and destroy every rival's army, town centers and military buildings. A completed Wonder that survives long enough also wins.

Economy

Villagers gather food (sheep, berries, deer, farms), wood, gold and stone, carrying it to a Town Center, Mill, Lumber Camp or Mining Camp. Build Houses to raise your population limit.

Military

Spearmen beat cavalry · archers beat infantry · skirmishers beat archers · knights beat archers and siege · rams destroy buildings · monks heal and convert. Research upgrades at the Blacksmith.

Mouse

Left click / dragSelect / box-select units
Double / Ctrl-clickSelect all of that type on screen
Right clickMove, gather, attack, build, garrison, set rally point
Shift + right clickQueue waypoints
Wheel / middle dragZoom / pan · screen edges and arrow keys also scroll
